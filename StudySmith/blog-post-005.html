HEC Degree Attestation 2026: New Digital System & What Changed

HEC's major 2026 update explained. New timelines, digital verification, and how to avoid rejection from foreign universities.

The Big Change: HEC's New Digital Attestation System (Effective April 2026)

In April 2026, HEC Pakistan launched a new digital attestation system that fundamentally changed how Pakistani degree certificates are verified by foreign universities. If you're applying to international universities in 2026-27, this directly affects you. Most students don't know about this yet, which is why delays are already happening.

Here's what changed: Previously, foreign universities contacted HEC through postal mail, emails went unanswered for weeks, and verification took 6-12 weeks minimum. Now, HEC's digital portal allows real-time verification. In theory, this should be faster. In reality, most students aren't using it correctly, so they're experiencing the same delays or worse.

What Actually Changed (The 5 Key Updates)

1. Online Attestation Portal Instead of Physical Letters

Old system: University requests attestation, HEC sends physical letterhead document. Takes 6-12 weeks. Documents get lost in transit.

New system (2026): Universities access HEC's online portal, upload your graduation documents, HEC verifies digitally within 2-3 weeks. Document is downloadable, not physical.

What this means for you: When you're applying to universities, provide the HEC attestation portal link instead of waiting for physical documents. This cuts waiting time in half.

2. "Degree Equivalence" Now Requires Pre-Registration

Important change: Starting 2026, if you're applying to countries that require degree equivalence (USA, UK, Australia, Canada), you MUST register your degree on HEC's new Equivalence Module before the university's verification request.

How it works: You go to HEC's website, upload your transcript and certificate, pay a small fee (PKR 2,000-3,000), and wait for HEC's initial verification. Once approved, foreign universities' requests process 10x faster because HEC already has your file ready.

Timeline impact: Without pre-registration: 8-12 weeks for equivalence. With pre-registration: 2-3 weeks after university's request.

3. Different Process for Punjab vs. Other Provinces

Critical update: Universities in Punjab province now have a separate, faster HEC attestation channel. If you graduated from COMSATS, UET, UCH, or other Punjab universities, your documents process 30% faster through the new Punjab Education Board integration.

If you graduated outside Punjab: Still use the national HEC portal, but timelines are slightly slower (2-3 weeks longer) because the system is still ramping up.

4. Degree Verification Now Includes Background Checks

New security measure: HEC now cross-references your degree against the university's actual graduation records and checks for any disciplinary issues or incomplete coursework. This is standard practice globally, but it's new in Pakistan.

What this means: If there are any discrepancies (missing subjects, incomplete grades, disciplinary records), HEC will flag these during verification. Foreign universities won't accept the attestation if flags exist.

Action item: Before applying internationally, pull your complete transcript from your university and verify every course is listed, every grade is correct, and you have no pending dues or disciplinary cases. If issues exist, resolve them BEFORE foreign universities request verification.

5. Gold Seal Attestations (For Australia, UK, USA)

New service: HEC offers a "Gold Seal" attestation for students applying to Commonwealth countries (Australia, UK, Canada) and the USA. This is a certified, secure document with blockchain verification.

Cost: PKR 5,000 extra (roughly $20 USD).

Why it matters: Some universities (especially UK Russell Group) are moving toward accepting only Gold Seal attestations to prevent forgery. If your target university mentions "HEC Gold Seal" or "certified attestation," get this version.

Timeline: Gold Seal takes 5-7 business days after application.

Step-by-Step: How to Get Your Degree Attested in 2026

Step 1: Check Your University's Requirement (1 day)

Email your target universities and ask specifically: "Which attestation method do you require? Do you accept HEC digital attestation, physical letters, or Gold Seal only?"

Different universities have different preferences. Some still want physical letters (this is changing but slowly). Most now accept digital. A few require Gold Seal. Know this before you invest time.

Step 2: Register on HEC's Equivalence Module (2-3 days)

Go to www.hec.gov.pk and navigate to the "Student Services" → "Equivalence Module."

Upload:

  • Your diploma/degree certificate (scanned, color, both sides)
  • Your complete transcript (all 8 semesters)
  • National ID (CNIC)
  • University ID (if you have it)

Pay PKR 2,500 online. Wait for HEC's confirmation (usually within 1-2 days).

Step 3: Provide HEC Reference Number to Universities (1 day)

Once HEC confirms your registration, you get a reference number. When universities request attestation, give them this reference number. This cuts processing time significantly because HEC doesn't have to verify your identity again.

Step 4: Wait for University's Attestation Request (Variable)

When you're offered admission (conditional on attestation), the university will submit a formal request to HEC. Now HEC processes it fast because they already have your pre-verified documents.

Timeline: 2-3 weeks (vs. 8-12 weeks in the old system).

Step 5: Download or Receive Attestation (1-2 days)

Once HEC completes verification, they'll notify you. You can download the digital attestation immediately and forward it to your university.

Optional: If you also want a physical Gold Seal copy, HEC can courier it to you (add 5-7 days + PKR 5,000).

Common Mistakes Students Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Mistake 1: Waiting Until University Asks for Attestation

What happens: Student gets admission offer. University requests attestation. Student then starts HEC process. By the time attestation arrives, student has 2 days to accept visa appointment. Visa gets delayed.

Fix: Register for HEC equivalence immediately after receiving admission offer, not after university requests it. This way, when the official request comes, it processes in 2-3 weeks instead of forcing 8-12 weeks of waiting.

Mistake 2: Not Mentioning Disciplinary Cases or Incomplete Courses

The trap: Student has a failed course or disciplinary record they forget to mention. HEC's new background check finds it. Attestation is delayed 2-3 weeks while HEC contacts the university to resolve the discrepancy.

Fix: Before registering on HEC's equivalence module, call your university registrar and ask: "Do I have any incomplete courses, failed grades, or disciplinary records?" If yes, get them resolved FIRST. This prevents delays later.

Mistake 3: Uploading Low-Quality Scans

What happens: Student uploads blurry certificate scan. HEC rejects it and asks for re-submission. 1 week delay.

Fix: Scan documents in color, both sides, high resolution (300 DPI). If scanning at home is poor quality, use your university's official document office. They'll provide certified scans.

Mistake 4: Not Getting Gold Seal for UK/Australia

The issue: Student gets digital attestation, sends it to UK university. University later says "We only accept Gold Seal attestations now." Student has to start the process again, causing 2-3 week delay.

Fix: Ask universities upfront if they require Gold Seal. If they do, order it immediately (PKR 5,000 extra, 5-7 days). If they don't require it but might in future, get it anyway. The small extra cost and time now saves major headaches later.

Timeline Reference (2026): When to Start

  • If applying in June-July 2026: Register for HEC equivalence by late May 2026. University requests in August-September. Attestation by October (in time for visa appointment November).
  • If applying in September-October 2026: Register for HEC equivalence by August 2026. University requests in November. Attestation by January (tight timeline, but doable).
  • If applying in January 2027: Register for HEC equivalence by November 2026. University requests in February. Attestation by April (comfortable timeline).

Email: attestation@hec.gov.pk

Online Portal: www.hec.gov.pk → Equivalence Module

Average response time: 1-2 business days for queries (huge improvement from 2-3 weeks in 2025).

Bottom Line: Don't Let Attestation Delay Your Visa

The new HEC digital system is faster — IF you use it correctly. Pre-register, upload quality documents, check for discrepancies, get Gold Seal if needed. Most delays now are student-caused, not HEC-caused. Plan ahead and you'll have attestation in 2-3 weeks, not 3 months.
